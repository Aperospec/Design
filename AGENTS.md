# Skill maintenance

Maintain the skill under `skills/` as an independent professional capability. Preserve actual methods and local reference integrity. Keep project facts, specific styles, formats, targets, budgets, permissions and case records outside the distributed skill. Generalized changes need a defined failure mechanism, scope, professional basis and relevant forward and counterexample checks. Do not introduce mandatory management layers or claim unperformed reviews.

Keep real test inputs and results in the maintenance project, not in the installable skill. Distribute only reviewed skill files and repository documentation.
